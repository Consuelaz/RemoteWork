Fed Survey: 87% of Market Contacts Now Flag AI as a Financial Stability Risk

美联储 11 月《金融稳定报告》引用纽约联储对 23 名市场联系人的调查：87% 的受访者将 AI 列为未来 12 至 18 个月的潜在金融稳定冲击来源，远高于春季调查的 50%。报告列举的担忧包括资产估值、网络安全，以及日益复杂的 AI 融资形式。这是监管层调查中首次有如此高比例的受访者把 AI 与系统性风险并列，意味着市场对 AI 的讨论正从增长机会延伸到估值、融资结构与网络安全的系统性关联，投资人会更加关注 AI 资本开支的回报兑现节奏与融资结构的脆弱性。

Concerns include asset valuations, cybersecurity, and increasingly complex AI financing structures. It is the first time such a high share of respondents has placed AI alongside systemic risks, signaling that investor focus is shifting from growth narratives toward capital-expenditure payback and financing fragility.

AI Developer Digest: Optimize the Loop, Not Just the Model

本期开发者文摘（11 月 14 日）聚焦三个工程信号。其一，Together AI 的 Together Compiler 论文（arXiv 2611.14681）把推理优化变成持续系统问题：分层持久引擎加成本模型让测量既快又具代表性，agent 自动搜索更优执行配置，Llama 1B 工作负载较手写 megakernel 提升最高 1.7 倍、部分前沿基准吞吐高 2.6 倍。其二，「清理 agent 历史记录反而有害」（arXiv 2611.14654）：当外部世界仍保留原始错误时，重写会话历史让恢复效果更差，而诚实的错误旁注反而提升恢复率——对话历史不是 ground truth，下游系统应校验真实世界状态而非信任漂亮的记录。其三，Zugwise 用国际象棋领域约束重建手写棋谱，在通用校验失效时用领域结构捕捉静默错误。共同结论：可靠的 AI 产品来自更好的反馈回路与真实状态追踪，而不是再叠一层模型调用。

This developer digest (Nov 14) highlights three engineering signals. First, Together AI's Together Compiler paper (arXiv 2611.14681) treats inference optimization as a continual systems problem: a tiered persistent engine plus cost model keeps measurement fast yet representative, with agents searching execution configurations - up to 1.7x over hand-written megakernels on Llama 1B. Second, cleaning an agent's history hurts when the world still holds the error (arXiv 2611.14654): rewriting transcripts reduces recovery, while a truthful note beside the preserved error improves it - conversation history is not ground truth. Third, Zugwise uses chess-specific constraints to catch silent transcription errors. The thread: reliable AI products come from better feedback loops and truthful state tracking, not another model call.
