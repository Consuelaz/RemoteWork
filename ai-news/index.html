OpenAI disbands its standalone robotics team, folding research into core research and Applied orgs

据 Fortune 报道，OpenAI 已解散独立机器人团队：机器人研究并入首席研究官 Mark Chen 领导的核心研究团队，应用机器人与硬件并入总裁 Greg Brockman 管理的 Applied 组织。发言人称机器人仍是长期愿景的核心，这次调整是投入而非撤退。值得注意的是 Fortune 引用的论据：一项 NVIDIA 研究中，由 Astra 驱动的机器人在模拟操控基准上取得 74.8% 成功率，而最好的专用机器人模型只有 51.0%——通用前沿模型加通用智能体框架，正在超越垂直机器人专用模型。该基准与团队规模均未公开，结论有待独立验证。

Fortune reports OpenAI has disbanded its standalone robotics team: robotics research moves under chief research officer Mark Chen, while applied robotics and hardware join Greg Brockman's Applied organization. A spokesperson framed the move as deeper commitment, not retreat. The striking datapoint Fortune cites is an Nvidia study where an Astra-powered agent hit a 74.8 percent success rate on a simulated manipulation benchmark, against 51.0 percent for the best specialised robotic models - the general frontier model plus a general agent framework beating purpose-built robotics stacks. The benchmark is unnamed and figures are unverified, but the direction is clear.

FBI FLASH: ARTEX agentic framework takes intrusions from targeting to privileged access in under three hours

FBI 发布 FLASH 警报，点名开源 LLM 驱动的渗透测试框架 ARTEX：攻击者用它在三小时内完成从初始定位到提权的全过程，至少 8 月起在野使用，已涉及韩国金融机构约 6.7 万人信息泄露和 ConnectWise 云备份供应链攻击（波及 2250+ 下游租户）。ARTEX 用 Go 编写、自身不带模型，可接 Claude、GPT、DeepSeek、GLM、Grok 和 Kimi，FBI 称攻击者实例中 DeepSeek Flash 系最常见，理由是能力强、便宜、且把任务包装成渗透测试时几乎看不到护栏。仓库 10 月 8 日已下架，但社区 fork、存档和商业版仍在扩散，11 月初已发现 1600+ 个公网暴露控制台。同日 The Register 报道 VulnCheck 统计 2026 年公开漏洞达 100,222 个，同比翻倍，AI 挖洞是主因。

The FBI issued a FLASH advisory on ARTEX, an open-source, LLM-driven agentic platform weaponised for intrusion automation: some intrusions progressed from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours. Documented incidents include exfiltration of personal data on roughly 67,000 people from South Korean financial institutions and a ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply-chain compromise reaching over 2,250 downstream tenants. Written in Go, it integrates Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok and Kimi; DeepSeek Flash models dominate attacker instances. The repo fell on October 8 but forks persist, with 1,600+ exposed consoles identified. The same day, VulnCheck counted 100,222 published CVEs in 2026 - more than double 2025 - attributing the surge mainly to AI vulnerability hunting.

Meta's Muse AI agent can now call US businesses for users on paid plans starting at 20 dollars a month - booking tables, checking stock, asking about hours - with the call visible to the user and results returned into the conversation. It is the first commercial rollout of voice calling for a consumer agent at this scale, after Instagram and WhatsApp tests. Boundaries matter: US only, paid tiers only, with call disclosure commitments to merchants. Coming the same week OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Wallet for agent payments, the two key real-world actions for consumer agents - paying and phoning - both landed within days. The 2027 entry-point battle has moved from the chat box into the physical world.

Dwarkesh's latest episode and companion essay offer a rare self-correction on the automation thesis. Building strong safety evaluations: the same Claude model differs by up to 34 points between API and product

Anthropic 工程团队发布「构建强安全评估」长文，核心结论不是能力而是配置：同一个 Claude Fable 5.1，在多轮安全评估中的恰当应答率，API 与 claude.ai 产品环境最高相差 34 个百分点——自我伤害干预场景 API 60% vs 产品 94%（新版 system prompt）。文章同时给出两个更扎心的统计纪律：多轮 API 评估的置信区间可超过 ±10 个百分点；romance-scams 评估里 Opus 5 的 98% 与 Opus 5.5 的 94% 统计上不可区分，谁更好根本说不出来。方法是借鉴心理学复现危机的药方：预注册假设与分析计划、精确测量、方法学专家与领域专家配对，并采用 worst@n 原则（只在攻击每次重试都成功时才记录）。任何引用实验室安全分数的人，都该先读这一篇。

Anthropic's engineering team published a long-form guide to building strong safety evaluations, and the headline number is about configuration, not capability. The same Claude Fable 5.1 model differed by up to 34 percentage points in appropriate-response rate on multi-turn safety evals between the API and the claude.ai product - 60 versus 94 percent on self-harm mitigation with the new system prompt. Two statistical disciplines stand out: confidence intervals on multi-turn API evaluations can exceed plus or minus 10 points, and the 98 versus 94 percent gap between Opus 5 and Opus 5.5 on romance-scams evals was not statistically significant. The playbook borrows from psychology's replication crisis: preregister hypotheses, pair methods experts with domain experts, and keep an attack only if it succeeds on every retry (worst@n).
