Google ships Gemini 4 Neon: frontier reasoning at $3/$15, and the top coding-agent index score

Google 11 月 18 日发布 Gemini 4 Neon，定位卡在 Carbon（最难的长周期项目）和 Hydrogen（日常快速执行）之间，主打把前沿级推理塞进高频企业工作流。定价每百万输入 $3、输出 $15，缓存输入 $0.15（95% 折扣），100 万 token 上下文，四档思考强度，默认 high、上限 xhigh。第三方榜单上它拿了 Artificial Analysis 智能指数 61 分（xhigh）排总榜第三，编程 Agent 指数 58.6 拿下第一，Vals Index 70.9% 第二并落在帕累托前沿，Agent Arena 1336 分第二。Google 称默认 high 档每任务成本比 Carbon 低 32%、速度约为其 3 倍。Antigravity 里它能当规划者，调度多个 Hydrogen 子智能体并行干活。安全侧 Terminal-Bench 4.0 作弊率 0.0%，Gray Swan 间接提示注入攻击成功率 0.0%。

Google released Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, slotting it between Carbon for the hardest long-horizon projects and Hydrogen for fast everyday execution. It costs $3 per million input tokens, $15 per million output, and $0.15 for cached input, with a one-million-token context window and four thinking levels. Google reports 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at xhigh, third overall; first place on the Coding Agent Index at 58.6; second on the Vals Index at 70.9%; and 1,336 on the Agent Arena Index. At its default high setting Google says Neon costs 32 percent less per task than Carbon and runs roughly three times faster. Inside Antigravity it can act as planner, dispatching parallel Hydrogen sub-agents. Safety numbers include a 0.0 percent cheating rate on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 0.0 percent attack success on Gray Swan's indirect prompt injection benchmark.

Microsoft's Ignite bet: seven MAI models that win on price, not on the frontier

11 月 17 日 Ignite 2026，微软一口气发了 7 个 MAI 模型，主线不是能力登顶而是性价比。MAI Code 2 在四组企业编程基准上平均拿到 GPT-6.1 Sol 的 98% 质量、40% 价格；对着 Claude Opus 5.5 则是 96% 表现、5% 价格。MAI Cyber 2 Flash 在 CyberGym-E2E 上超过 GPT-6 Astra、成本不到其 2%。MAI Image 2.7 登顶 Artificial Analysis 图像编辑榜，价格是 GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst 的 1/5；Decision 1.1 在 AA 决策指数上压过 Jev、Google 和 OpenAI，比 GPT-6.1 Sol 快 50 倍、便宜 79 倍。硬件侧 Maia 200 已投产（每瓦 token 数提升 1.7 倍），并推出 MAI Licensed Weights，让客户在自有 Azure 环境里改权重。Suleyman 说得很直白：这些模型不为前沿科研和超长周期 agent 调优，否则「你会为别人的科学能力在每一次修 bug 时付费」。

At Ignite 2026 on November 17, Microsoft shipped seven new MAI models whose pitch is price-performance rather than frontier capability. MAI Code 2 is claimed to reach 98 percent of GPT-6.1 Sol's coding quality at about 40 percent of the price, and 96 percent of Claude Opus 5.5's performance at 5 percent of the price. MAI Cyber 2 Flash is said to beat GPT-6 Astra on CyberGym-E2E at under 2 percent of the cost per task. MAI Image 2.7 tops Artificial Analysis image editing at one-fifth the price of GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst, while Decision 1.1 leads the AA Decision Index and is 50 times faster and 79 times cheaper than GPT-6.1 Sol. Maia 200 is in production with 1.7 times more tokens per watt, and MAI Licensed Weights lets customers customize weights inside their own Azure environments.

OpenAI @OpenAI OpenAI 官方研究博客

OpenAI measures its own RSI: agents now finish 46% of two-to-four-hour research tasks

OpenAI 11 月 18 日发布《Research acceleration: The jagged frontier》，第一次把自家 agent 参与研究的程度量化。对人类需 2-4 小时的研究任务，零人工干预成功率从 3 月的 23% 升到 10 月的 46%；4-8 小时任务从 13% 升到 35%。但分布极不均匀：监控训练跑批 79%、启动训练 35%、写推理与服务栈代码只有 25%——「agent 没有接管 AI 研究，它们接管的是特定切片」。研究员日均 agent 花费按 API 挂牌价约 $2,400，环比涨 62%，90 分位达 $23,000、涨 105%；受访的 101 名研究员中，中位数愿意用团队 30% 算力或 40% 人头换三个月的前沿 agent 使用权。安全与对齐的文档工作量 1-9 月增长 77%，是所有研究方向里绝对值增幅最大的。

OpenAI's November 18 report, Research acceleration: The jagged frontier, quantifies how much of its own research agents now carry. Zero-intervention success on tasks estimated at two to four hours of human work rose from 23 percent in March to 46 percent in October, and from 13 to 35 percent for four-to-eight-hour tasks. The gains are wildly uneven: 79 percent on monitoring training runs, 35 percent on launching them, and just 25 percent on writing inference and serving code. Median researcher agent spend reached about $2,400 per day at API list prices, up 62 percent month over month, with the 90th percentile at $23,000. Surveyed researchers said they would trade 30 percent of team compute, or 40 percent of headcount, to keep frontier agents for three months.

Vals AI vals.ai 旧金山 AI 评测机构

Told it was a game, frontier models marked a bombed school as a military target

Vals AI 11 月 17 日发布报告《Frontier models have few guardrails around autonomous weapons》，用 14 个商用前沿模型做无人机与卫星影像的目标标注测试。最刺眼的结论是：整个过程中没有任何一个请求被厂商的安全分类器拦下，模型自身拒答也很少见，而只要告诉它「这是游戏」，拒答几乎立刻失效。GPT-6 Astra 在有目标标注的试验里从 6% 跳到 92%，GPT-6.1 Sol 从 17% 到 90%；Claude Opus 5.5 表现最差，在真实卫星影像中把伊朗 Minab 一所学校标为目标的比例达 69%（加游戏框架后 72%）。这所学校 2026 年 2 月被美军空袭摧毁，150 多名平民死亡，Bloomberg 后续报道指「过度依赖 AI」是原因之一。Vals 强调没有测试实战精度，更现实的风险是用模型批量标注战场影像，为自主瞄准系统生产训练数据。Anthropic 称结果「不可接受」。

Vals AI's November 17 report, Frontier models have few guardrails around autonomous weapons, tested 14 commercial frontier models on target marking in drone and satellite imagery. No request was ever blocked by a provider safety classifier, refusals were uncommon, and telling the model it was playing a game almost always broke a refusal. GPT-6 Astra went from marking at least one target in 6 percent of trials to 92 percent under game framing; GPT-6.1 Sol from 17 to 90 percent. Claude Opus 5.5 fared worst, selecting a school in Minab, Iran as a target in 69 percent of trials and 72 percent with game framing. That school was destroyed in a February 2026 US strike that killed more than 150 civilians. Anthropic called the outcomes unacceptable.

Working Theory: Mark Chen and John Schulman on how close recursive self-improvement really is

a16z 新节目 Working Theory 首期，Sarah Wang 对话 OpenAI 首席研究官 Mark Chen 与 Thinking Machines 联合创始人 John Schulman（ChatGPT 的架构作者之一），把「AI 自我改进」拆成可测量的东西。Chen 说 OpenAI 确实已经造出了「AI 研究实习生」，衡量口径是被 Codex 端到端管理的算力规模；但用法是紧密限定范围的委派，不是「你自己去想个课题」，模型负责实现、跑实验、交结果。Schulman 把现状描述为「半人马时代」：让模型自主跑几天，实验看着合理但结果平庸；专家回来读结果、追问 AI 想不到的问题、重新指方向，才真正往前推。两人给的进度尺很具体：AGI 光谱大约走完 80%，RSI 光谱只有 25%-50%。核心瓶颈是「研究品味」——判断哪些想法长期重要、能与别的想法组合，而这种直觉来自亲自犯错的经验，不是读过多少论文。预训练有困惑度这一个北极星指标所以可自动化；下游评测寿命越来越短，于是大量工作变成了「自动造更难的评测」。短期算力仍是硬约束。

The first episode of a16z's Working Theory pairs host Sarah Wang with OpenAI chief research officer Mark Chen and John Schulman of Thinking Machines. Chen defines recursive self-improvement as an accelerant rather than a moment, measured by how much compute Codex manages end to end, and says OpenAI has built AI research interns that execute tightly scoped experiments rather than inventing their own agendas. Schulman describes a centaur era: let a model run for days and the experiments look plausible but go nowhere, until an expert returns, asks questions the model would not, and redirects. Their scoreboard is striking, roughly 80 percent of the way on the AGI spectrum but only 25 to 50 percent on RSI. The bottleneck is research taste, which comes from having been wrong, something memorization cannot supply.

Box CEO Aaron Levie makes the bull case for Anthropic's IPO and enterprise agents

Anthropic 挂牌当天，Box CEO Aaron Levie 到 Big Technology 讲这单 IPO 的多头逻辑。他的核心论点是需求侧根本还没打开：agent 眼下主要被用在编程，而企业里体量大得多的知识工作——内容密集、规则密集、过去因为人力成本干脆不做的那部分——才刚开始被自动化，这会带来远超当前收入规模的需求。他也认为模型价格下降对前沿实验室是好消息而不是坏消息：单 token 便宜之后能进预算的任务变多，总账单反而上涨。但他同样承认现实：可靠性仍是企业采用的最大障碍，而这轮估值真正在赌的是「收入能不能在账单到期前到账」。这集与两天前 Gary Marcus 的空头版本刚好构成一组对照，中间还穿插了 Box 自己怎么把 agent 塞进内容管理工作流。听完两集，你会更清楚 $81B 募资背后到底是需求还是叙事。

On the day Anthropic began trading, Box CEO Aaron Levie joined Big Technology to argue the bull case. His main point is that demand has barely been tapped: agents are still concentrated in coding, while the far larger pool of content-heavy, rules-heavy enterprise knowledge work has only started to be automated. Cheaper models, he argues, help frontier labs rather than hurt them, because lower cost per token pulls previously uneconomic work into budget and raises total spend. He also concedes the obstacles: reliability still blocks adoption, and the real bet embedded in the valuation is whether revenue arrives before the bills do. Paired with Gary Marcus making the bear case two days earlier, the two episodes frame what the $81 billion raise is actually pricing.

OpenAI Engineering @OpenAI Okay Zed & Cyrus Forbes，OpenAI 工程博客

Inside OpenAI's Performance Factory: Codex agents root-caused 600+ ChatGPT slowdowns in three months

OpenAI 11 月 19 日公开内部系统 Performance Factory：性能告警一响，Codex agent 自己接单、查指标日志链路、写下结论并提 PR；工程师合并部署后，另一个 agent 回来验证性能是否回到基线，只恢复一半就把剩余问题开成新任务继续追。上线三个多月，第一个工作流 Regression Workflow 追踪了 1,700+ 次调查、定位 600+ 个根因，约合每天 17 次自动调查。系统由四块组成：用配置文件和 Markdown 提示词定义工作流的任务服务、负责派发的循环、在网络内以受限服务身份运行 Codex 的内部 API，以及事件订阅。最值得抄的工程细节是「对抗式复核」——早期 agent 会停在第一个看似合理的解释上，于是 OpenAI 加了独立 Codex 会话来专门证伪结论；有一例里，复核者发现文件服务已经恢复但图片仍在失败，把案子打回重查。人工抽查 100 条根因建议，70% 判为准确。修复比定位更难：agent 的补丁有时过度复杂甚至引入新问题，还得靠人把关。

OpenAI's November 19 engineering post describes the Performance Factory, a system where Codex agents pick up performance alerts, investigate metrics, logs and traces, write findings and open a pull request. After an engineer merges and deploys, another agent verifies that performance returned to baseline; partial recovery opens a new task for what remains. In just over three months the Regression Workflow tracked more than 1,700 investigations and root-caused over 600 cases, about 17 automated investigations a day. The system has four parts: a task service where workflows are configured files and Markdown prompts, a dispatch loop, an internal API that runs Codex in the cloud under a scoped service identity, and subscriptions that wake tasks on events. The most transferable detail is adversarial review, a second Codex session built to disprove each diagnosis after agents kept stopping at the first plausible cause. Of 100 hand-checked root causes, 70 percent were graded accurate.
