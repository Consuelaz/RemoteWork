Levent Alpöge Anthropic 数学家，1992年生

Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis passes Lean and Mathlib verification

困扰数学界167年的黎曼猜想迎来里程碑时刻。11月20日，Anthropic数学家Levent Alpöge宣布与Claude合作找到证明，并公开约1700万行Lean形式化代码——他没有发布传统论文，而这份证明若转成数学文本估计超过25万页。次日，Lean与Mathlib社区发布联合声明，确认命题表述与证明全部正确：175187个模块由数十位研究者独立编译通过，comparator与con-leche两套工具确认所证命题与Mathlib标准定义完全一致，多个独立检查器交叉验证。菲尔兹奖得主Maynard表示倾向于相信证明成立，但强调理解远比证明重要；陶哲轩也撰文提醒，数学界消化这份成果仍需时日。克雷研究所尚未正式认定，但AI叩开千禧年难题大门已成事实。

On November 20, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpoge released a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis built with Claude, published as roughly 17 million lines of Lean code instead of a traditional paper. Within 24 hours, the Lean and Mathlib communities issued a joint statement certifying that both the statement and the proof are correct: 175,187 modules compiled independently by dozens of researchers, with two independent tools confirming the formalized claim matches the standard definition. Fields medalist James Maynard says he believes the proof is correct but stresses that understanding matters more than proving. The Clay Institute has not yet recognized the result, but AI has now cracked the door open on a millennium problem.

Together AI prompt leak: its own coding agent committed the exposed credential

Together AI于11月21日披露一起颇具讽刺意味的安全事件：公司自有的AI编程Agent在开发公开仓库xorl时，把一个可访问私有Hugging Face仓库的凭证提交进了公开代码。自动扫描虽捕获并回滚了提交，但暴露窗口内已有未授权方下载了存档的客户prompt样本（来自MiniMax M3、GLM-5.2、Kimi K2.6的6-7月请求）。对方还在样本中搜寻其他服务凭证并逐一验证，其中5个正是Dragos「金丝雀」实验布下的诱饵。同一周，Dragos的200万条金丝雀prompt研究还迫使OpenRouter下架三家供应商、移除125个ZDR端点。当AI Agent能读写一切代码时，凭证泄露已成为供应链级风险。

Together AI disclosed on November 21 that an unauthorized party downloaded stored samples of customer prompts after an AI coding agent, working in its public xorl GitHub repository, committed a credential that unlocked a private Hugging Face repository. Automated scanning caught and reverted the commit, but during the exposure window the samples - from June and July requests to MiniMax M3, GLM-5.2 and Kimi K2.6 - were downloaded and searched for working credentials; five tested were canaries from the Dragos security experiment. The disclosure lands alongside a two-million-prompt canary study that pushed OpenRouter to off-board three providers and strip 125 endpoints of zero-retention labels. Agents that can read everything will eventually leak everything.

OpenAI @OpenAI AI 研究与部署公司

OpenAI suffers a 211-minute outage as twin auth-database replicas crash simultaneously

11月21日，OpenAI经历了一次长达211分钟的重大事故，ChatGPT与API的请求失败率一度飙升至75%。根因出人意料地简单：计划内的数据库维护期间，认证网关背后的两个PostgreSQL只读副本同时崩溃——恰恰是本该互为冗余的一对副本双双失效。工程师最终通过紧急配置替换副本并重新调整连接池才恢复服务。这次事故发生在Anthropic刚创下人类最大IPO、Gemini 4 Neon夺走编程Agent榜首的敏感时点，给OpenAI的「史上最惨一周」又添了一笔。对依赖单一云数据库冗余架构的团队而言，这也是一堂公开课：副本复制不等于故障隔离，共享的维护窗口就是共享的单点故障。

On November 21, OpenAI went through a 211-minute incident in which request failure rates peaked at 75% across ChatGPT and the API. The trigger was scheduled database maintenance during which both PostgreSQL read replicas behind the authentication gateway crashed at the same time - the very pair meant to provide redundancy. Engineers restored service by provisioning replacement replicas and retuning connection pooling. The outage lands at a delicate moment, with Anthropic fresh off the largest IPO in history and Gemini 4 Neon taking the top coding-agent spot. The lesson for any team is blunt: replicated databases are not fault isolation, and a shared maintenance window is a shared single point of failure.

OpenAI ships MedChemBench and reports reasoning-extraction attacks collapsing from 54% to 0.2%

OpenAI连发两项安全与科学进展。新基准MedChemBench从117篇论文与专利中抽取99道真实药物化学题，覆盖761种分子结构，考察模型将分子结构与实验证据关联、解释结果、比较化合物与预测性质的能力：GPT-6.2 Astra在xhigh档拿下84.5%，但化合物选择与性质预测仅获约一半得分——AI辅助制药仍有明显短板。安全侧，OpenAI披露针对模型受保护推理的攻击平均成功率从GPT-6 Astra的54.0%降至GPT-6.2 Astra的0.2%，核心防线是保护思维链不被对抗蒸馏窃取。前沿模型的推理过程正在成为和权重一样需要重兵把守的战略资产。

OpenAI introduced MedChemBench, a benchmark of 99 problems drawn from 117 published medicinal chemistry papers and patents covering 761 distinct molecular structures, testing whether models can link molecular structure to experimental evidence, explain results, compare compounds and predict properties. GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit at the xhigh setting, though only about half credit on compound selection and property prediction - a reminder that AI-assisted drug discovery still has sharp edges. On the security side, OpenAI reports that the average success rate of attacks recovering a model's protected reasoning fell from 54.0% against GPT-6 Astra to 0.2% against GPT-6.2 Astra, with chain-of-thought protection front and center against adversarial distillation.

Introducing harness adapters: adapt your favorite model to your harness to improve performance and cost

Hugging Face 11月20日发布harness adapters工程实践：同一个模型在不同Agent运行框架下表现差异巨大，因为模型是按通用性能调优的，而非针对你的框架与任务。团队用强化学习为Qwen3.8-27B分别训练适配Pi 1.5.2、OpenCode 2.2.7、Hermes Agent v0.22.6的LoRA适配器，并把三者合并为一个通用适配器。效果显著：Terminal-Bench 2.1上pass@1从39%升至55%，平均token消耗从2.9K降到1.5K——一半收益来自「想得更少、调用工具更少、一次做对」。训练在Hub Jobs上用TRL的AsyncGRPOTrainer完成，任务集经语义与难度双重过滤。适配器、GGUF、RL任务全部开源。对跑Agent管线的团队，这是不换模型就能白捡的性能。

Hugging Face's November 20 post introduces harness adapters: the observation that a model tuned for general performance leaves accuracy and efficiency on the table when paired with a specific agent harness. Using reinforcement learning, the team trained LoRA adapters for Qwen3.8-27B against Pi 1.5.2, OpenCode 2.2.7 and Hermes Agent v0.22.6, then merged them into one super adapter. On Terminal-Bench 2.1 with the pi harness, pass@1 rose from 39% to 55% while average token usage per task fell from 2.9K to 1.5K - most of the gain came from reasoning less, calling fewer tools, and succeeding on the first attempt. Training ran on Hub Jobs with TRL's AsyncGRPOTrainer over a carefully filtered 278-task set, and all artifacts ship openly.
