Anthropic & OpenAI @AnthropicAI @OpenAI 两大前沿实验室同日发布数学证明预印本

Anthropic and OpenAI both claim AI-written proofs that VP is not equal to VNP, formalized in Lean 4

两大实验室在约四小时内先后发布预印本，宣称旗下模型独立证明了算术复杂度核心难题 VP 不等于 VNP——这是 P vs NP 千禧年难题的关键一步。Anthropic 称证明由内部研究版 Claude 产出：模型写出论文、完成数学论证，并自动转写为 Lean 4 形式化代码供机器逐步校验；OpenAI 的论文同样附带了形式化证明，两条路线细节有同有异，恰是独立工作的理想特征。剑桥学者评价其「远超数学家既有技术」。两家均未宣称解决了经典 P vs NP，且尚无数学家完整人工核验，结果仍待同行评审。

Roughly four hours apart, Anthropic and OpenAI both published preprints claiming their models independently proved VP is not equal to VNP, a major step inside the P vs NP Millennium Problem. Anthropic says an internal research version of Claude wrote the argument, drafted the paper, and converted it into Lean 4 formal code so a machine could verify each inference step; OpenAI's paper also includes a formalized proof. The two approaches resemble each other in places and diverge at key steps, exactly what independent work should look like. Neither company claims P vs NP itself is solved, no mathematician has fully verified either proof by hand, and expert reaction from Cambridge and Oxford remains measured but serious.

Dario Amodei fires back at Axios report on handing decision-making to superintelligence, calling it quote-cherry-picking

针对 Axios 报道称 Anthropic 倾向在超级智能出现后将决策权移交给它，Dario 罕见动怒发文反击。他驳斥这是断章取义，指出舆论混淆了「移交人类统治权」与「具体任务自动化」两个完全不同的概念：Anthropic 主张的是让 AI 自主完成受限的执行类任务，而非放弃人类主导权。他同时炮轰部分高喊「人类主导」口号的竞争对手，指出其产品在自动化路线上并无二致，实为商业「口号秀」。这场安全叙事之争再度升级，也折射出头部实验室在 AGI 路线上的深层分歧。

Responding to an Axios report that Anthropic leans toward handing decision-making authority to superintelligence, Dario Amodei published a rare, visibly angry rebuttal. He accused the report of cherry-picking quotes and conflating two very different ideas: ceding human rule to AI versus automating bounded, concrete tasks. Amodei argues Anthropic's position has always been AI autonomously executing constrained work, never surrendering human oversight. He also took aim at rivals who loudly champion human control while shipping products on essentially the same automation trajectory, calling it marketing theater. The exchange escalates the safety-narrative war between frontier labs and exposes a deeper strategic rift over AGI roadmaps.

Google Workspace @GoogleWorkspace Workspace MCP 服务与 CLI 正式 GA

Google makes Workspace MCP servers and CLI generally available, enforcing agent access via MCP from February 2027

Google 宣布 Workspace MCP 服务器与官方 CLI 正式可用，覆盖 Gmail、Drive、Calendar 等 8 大服务，并给出明确迁移截止线：2027 年 2 月 1 日起，AI Agent 将强制通过 MCP 访问 Workspace，取代现有的 REST API 直连方式。管理员可以为敏感操作设置确认要求，并审计 Agent 活动。这意味着 MCP 从社区协议正式升级为 Google 的组织级基础设施——数十亿账户规模的生态切换协议栈，对整个 Agent 开发生态是一次强信号：企业级 Agent 接入正在被协议化、标准化。

Google announced that its Workspace MCP servers and official CLI are now generally available, covering eight services including Gmail, Drive and Calendar, with a hard migration deadline: from February 1, 2027, AI agents will be required to access Workspace through MCP, replacing direct REST API access. Admins gain the ability to require confirmation for sensitive actions and to audit agent activity. The move elevates MCP from a community protocol to Google-scale organizational infrastructure, and it signals to the entire agent development ecosystem that enterprise agent access is being formalized and standardized at a scale of billions of accounts.

Koray Kavukcuoglu @kavukcuoglu Google DeepMind 研究副总裁，Gemini 负责人

DeepMind podcast: agents now schedule and run experiments for the next Gemini, but RSI is still far

在 11 月 26 日上线的 DeepMind 官方播客中，Gemini 研发负责人 Koray Kavukcuoglu 披露了实验室工作方式的根本转变：AI Agent 已在为下一代 Gemini 调度并执行部分研究实验，自主决定部分研究算力的分配——这是真实的研究参与，但他强调距离递归自我改进仍很遥远。组织层面的变化同样值得关注：DeepMind 不再为每个项目组建专属团队，而是用通用算法并行推进科学与产品。两个工程细节极具参考价值：训练时刻意不奖励思维链，以免模型为奖励信号优化内心独白，让意图保持可审计；他同时区分了「渲染」与「理解」——模型能生成视觉逼真的流体，却不代表掌握背后的物理。

On DeepMind's official podcast published November 26, Gemini research lead Koray Kavukcuoglu described a structural shift in how the lab builds models: AI agents now schedule and run some of the experiments behind the next generation of Gemini, deciding how to allocate compute for parts of the research process. He calls this real participation in research work while insisting the lab remains far from recursive self-improvement. Two engineering details stand out: DeepMind deliberately does not reward chain-of-thought during training, so intent stays auditable in natural language, and he draws a line between rendering and understanding, noting models produce convincing fluid simulations without evidence they grasp the underlying physics.
