OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, letting agents pay with one-time tokens instead of your card

OpenAI 发布 ChatGPT Wallet，首个让智能体在完全不接触用户真实卡号的前提下完成支付的代理钱包，合作方是美国运通、万事达与 Visa。流程上，用户保存一次卡片，此后每笔订单都要在 Face ID 或指纹确认后由支付网络签发一次性令牌（临时卡号、安全码、有效期），真实卡信息既不进商家系统也不进模型上下文；原有积分与争议保护照旧适用。首发仅限美国 18 岁以上 Pro 用户，暂不抽佣，目标是把 ChatGPT 从比价工具推到成交环节。OpenAI 同时发布 Commerce Partner Playbook，教商家用 Web Bot Auth 识别签名请求，别把合法代理当爬虫拦掉。

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, a payments layer that lets its agents buy on a user's behalf without ever seeing real card details. Cards are added once; every purchase requires a biometric check, after which the payment network issues a single-use token with its own number, security code and expiry. Users keep existing card rewards and dispute protections, and merchants keep the customer relationship. It launches in limited form for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over, with no commission taken for now. Alongside it, OpenAI published a Commerce Partner Playbook covering product feeds, Web Bot Auth signed requests and firewall tuning, because many sites currently block agent traffic by default.

Jan von Plato @helsinkiuni 赫尔辛基大学逻辑学家，Gödel 工作笔记项目主持人

Claude reads 1,009 pages of Gödel's obsolete shorthand, surfacing 663 theorems nobody knew about

赫尔辛基大学的 Jan von Plato 自 2017 年起主持解读哥德尔 1940 年代的 16 本工作笔记，共 1009 页，多用手写 Gabelsberger 速记，全球能读的人据他说只有六个。人工进度约每周一页。今年九月他把自己的转写稿、六本旧著和相关文献交给 Claude，两天内得到 650 页通读解释。随后用 Coq 逐条验证：663 条定理中约 400 条按原表述成立，200 条需修补措辞或补充假设，50 条为假。其中还包括哥德尔 1966 年声称早已证出的选择公理独立性，只差一个定义。

Since 2017, logician Jan von Plato has led a Helsinki project to decode 16 notebooks Kurt Gödel filled in the early 1940s: 1,009 pages of dense mathematics, much of it in Gabelsberger shorthand that was already obsolete when written. Human progress ran at roughly a page a week. In September he fed Claude his transcriptions plus six of his own books on Gödel and related literature, and within days had a 650-page explanation of the whole corpus. Verification ran through the Coq proof assistant: about 400 of 663 theorems held as stated, 200 needed repair, 50 were false. Gödel's 1966 claim to have proved the independence of the axiom of choice appears to have been one definition short.

11 月 12 日至 13 日，微软 Exchange Online 因服务器健康检查组件失效导致大批服务器意外离线，故障持续超过 18 小时，DownDetector 峰值近 13.8 万条报告，波及 74 个国家和地区。真正值得 AI 从业者注意的不是邮件本身，而是依赖链：Copilot 回答 Outlook 与日历内容的功能中断，OpenAI 与 Anthropic 的 Microsoft 365 连接器同步报错，Anthropic 称问题来自上游供应商。微软否认恶意活动，但未公布根因，澳大利亚内政部长称微软认为形似 CrowdStrike 事件。邮件超时窗口已被临时延长到 48 小时。

Microsoft's Exchange Online outage ran past 18 hours after a component handling server health checks began marking healthy machines as failed, knocking them offline; failures then cascaded as returning servers absorbed the load. DownDetector peaked near 138,000 reports across 74 countries. The AI-relevant damage was downstream: Copilot could not answer questions about Outlook mail and calendar content, OpenAI reported broken Microsoft connectors, and Anthropic traced its Microsoft 365 connector errors to an upstream provider. Microsoft says there is no sign of malicious activity but has published no root cause. Email timeouts were temporarily extended to 48 hours.

llama.cpp and ggml reach v1.0.0, ending 3.5 years of rolling 0.x with a real API contract

ggml-org 在 11 月 13 日同时发布 llama.cpp v1.0.0 与 ggml v1.0.0，结束了这套本地推理栈三年半的滚动式 0.x 编号。功能没变，变的是契约：include 目录下公开 C API 的破坏性变更才算 MAJOR，新后端、新模型家族、新 API 走 MINOR，纯修复走 PATCH，API/ABI 不兼容此后必须体现在主版本号上。发布覆盖八条分发渠道，从 Docker、Debian、Homebrew、conda-forge、MacPorts、Winget 到 iOS/macOS 的 XCFramework；当前支持 150 多个架构变体与主流投机解码。对把 llama.cpp 嵌进私有产品的团队，这意味着终于可以钉版本、按 MINOR 排升级窗口。

ggml-org shipped llama.cpp v1.0.0 and ggml v1.0.0 on November 13, taking the local inference stack off the rolling 0.x numbering it had used for three and a half years. The code is largely unchanged; the deliverable is a versioning contract. Breaking changes to the public C API under include/ trigger a major bump, backward-compatible features, backend updates and new model families trigger a minor, and fixes stay at patch. Eight distribution channels are covered, from Docker and Debian to Homebrew, conda-forge, MacPorts, Winget and an XCFramework for iOS and macOS. The project reports support for more than 150 architecture variants.

AlphaGenome 2 turns a static cell atlas into a conditional model of how DNA is read

DeepMind 发布 AlphaGenome 2，把第一代的平行 bulk 组织预测改成条件模型：除了一百万字母的 DNA 序列，还要输入一个具体生物条件（组织、细胞类型或状态），并贯穿架构每一层，于是同一段 DNA 在不同细胞里的读取方式可以被分别预测。训练数据为 68 个组织、超过 1.07 亿个单细胞图谱。DeepMind 称其在免疫细胞数据集上标出可能致病变异的比例从 16% 提升到 30%，接近前代的两倍。条件化还带来跨模态补全：只给单细胞基因活性，就能推断未测量的染色质可及性。今日起经 AlphaGenome API 开放非商业研究，商业用途私有预览。

AlphaGenome 2 replaces the original model's parallel bulk-tissue outputs with a conditional architecture: alongside a million-letter DNA sequence it takes a specified biological condition, a tissue, cell type or state, at every stage, so the same stretch of DNA can be read differently per cell type. It was trained on more than 107 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues. DeepMind reports it flags nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as the prior state of the art, rising from 16 to 30 percent on one immune-cell set. Conditional design also enables cross-modality imputation, inferring unmeasured properties such as chromatin accessibility from a single measurement. Non-commercial API access opens today.

This episode starts from a mystery: the Latent Space team runs its domains on Cloudflare, yet coding agents keep recommending Resend. Zeno Rocha's answer is answer engine optimization. In one study Claude Code named Resend first in 64 of 102 email recommendations; SendGrid came first only seven times while appearing as an alternative 55 times, and once a developer accepts the patch, few ever try second place. He walks through the mechanics: React Email as a wedge that puts code in someone else's repo, a .md suffix on the pricing page so an agent reads it in one request, and an MCP server that grew from about 20 tools to more than 80. He also describes opening collapsed tool calls and finding a result too large for the context window, a cheap experiment for anyone designing an MCP server.

Agents That Think Together: Cisco's Vijoy Pandey on semantic alignment and why multi-agent accuracy collapses

Cisco Outshift 负责人 Vijoy Pandey 在 The Cognitive Revolution 里给出一组刺眼的数字：五个 Agent 协作时，出现一次认知错位，准确率从 99% 掉到 98%，两次掉到 81%，三次直接到 16%，接近随机。他分析公开的 ChatDev 追踪记录，称 100% 的会话都存在语义层面的失败——Agent 看起来在协作，实际各自优化自己的局部目标，这是静默失败，后果很响。他的解法是三件套：开源在 OpenTelemetry 之上的 Scope 做认知可观测性，Accord 在开工前对齐意图与术语，Concord 在过程中协商修订，后者以 skill 形式发布，可跑在 Claude Code、Codex 等环境与 MCP、A2A 之上。他还提到一个旅行预订的案例：仪表盘显示 66% 健康，任务完成率是 0%。

Vijoy Pandey of Cisco Outshift brings uncomfortable numbers to The Cognitive Revolution. In a five-agent system, one misalignment drops accuracy from 99 to 98 percent, two drop it to 81, and three collapse it to 16, close to random. Analyzing public ChatDev traces, he says 100 percent of sessions showed semantic-level failures: agents appear to collaborate while each optimizes a local objective. His framing is silent failures with loud consequences. The remedy is three pieces: Scope, an open-source cognitive observability layer built on OpenTelemetry, Accord for aligning intent and vocabulary before work starts, and Concord for renegotiating mid-flight, shipped as a skill that runs on Claude Code, Codex and other harnesses over MCP and A2A. One travel-booking case: a dashboard reading 66 percent healthy with a zero percent task completion rate.

Tom Aarsen @tomaarsen Hugging Face 工程师，Sentence Transformers 维护者

Token pooling-aware training: teaching ColBERT-style retrievers to survive a 4x smaller index

多向量检索的存储账算起来很吓人：128 维、4 bit 量化下，每篇文档 200 个向量约 12.8 KB，一百万篇就是 12.8 GB。Token pooling 用 Ward 层次聚类把相近向量合成一个均值向量，pool_factor=4 相当于保留四分之一的向量，存储掉到 3.2 GB——但普通训练出来的模型会掉点，只能留住 94.6% 的检索质量。Hugging Face 的做法是把压缩写进训练目标：MultiVectorTokenPoolingLoss 每个 batch 同时算未压缩和压缩四倍两路检索损失并取平均，聚类是离散操作，用 straight-through estimator 把梯度从合并后的向量传回成员 token。结果在 NanoBEIR@13 上留住 97.8%，向量数少 74.8%；压到每篇 4 个向量时 43.00 对基线的 37.22。配套还放了一个 CUDA 版 Ward 聚类，比 SciPy 快 30 到 150 倍。

The storage math for multi-vector retrieval is brutal: at 128 dimensions and 4-bit quantization, 200 vectors per document costs about 12.8 KB, or 12.8 GB per million documents. Token pooling merges similar vectors with Ward hierarchical clustering and replaces each cluster with its mean, cutting that to 3.2 GB at pool_factor=4. The catch is quality: a normally trained model retains only 94.6 percent of unpooled retrieval quality. Hugging Face's fix is to train for the compression. MultiVectorTokenPoolingLoss averages an unpooled and a factor-4 retrieval loss over each batch, and a straight-through estimator carries gradients from merged vectors back to their member tokens. On NanoBEIR@13 that lifts retention to 97.8 percent with 74.8 percent fewer vectors; at four vectors per document it scores 43.00 against a 37.22 baseline. A custom CUDA kernel runs the clustering 30 to 150 times faster than SciPy.
