GPT-6 Cyber launches: 948 Linux kernel flaws found, 588 previously unknown, bug bounties quadrupled

The model finds and validates exploitable vulnerabilities, develops exploits and proposes patches while staying within researcher-defined rules of engagement. In a 30-hour autonomous run, an upcoming cyber model identified 948 local privilege-escalation flaws in the Linux kernel - 588 of them previously unknown - at roughly $1.2M in API costs. OpenAI also quadrupled bug bounty payouts to as much as $172K per exceptional finding. A frontier lab openly shipping offensive-capable security tooling marks a new phase for defenders and attackers alike.

OpenAI closes $11B round at $1.25T valuation, pledges grid costs and transparency after data center backlash

OpenAI 宣布完成 110 亿美元新一轮融资，估值达 1.25 万亿美元，与 GPT-6 Cyber 发布落在同一周。此前中期选举中，41 项数据中心地方公投有 40 项遭选民否决，社区阻力成为 AI 基础设施扩张的真实约束。作为回应，OpenAI 承诺终止与地方官员签署的保密协议、公开 Compute Campus 政策与国家社区契约、承担电网与基础设施成本，并签署电网灵活性协议——例如允许佐治亚电力在电网紧张时将其园区负载削减 1000 兆瓦。公司同时把数据中心更名为更友好的计算园区，凸显公众参与已成前沿实验室的必修课。

OpenAI closed $11 billion in new funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation, the same week it shipped GPT-6 Cyber and announced new data center commitments. The round lands after voters rejected data center projects in 40 of 41 local ballot measures, making community opposition a real constraint on AI infrastructure buildout. In response, OpenAI committed to ending NDAs with public officials, publishing a Compute Campus Policy and National Community Compact, and paying for electricity and infrastructure costs. It also agreed to grid flexibility deals, letting Georgia Power cut its campus load by 1,000 MW during grid stress, and rebranded its data centers as compute campuses. Public buy-in is now table stakes for frontier labs.

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash goes GA: 15-second video with native audio at 30% lower prices

Google DeepMind 在 AI Studio 和 Gemini API 中正式全面开放 Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash，取代 gemini-omni-1.1-flash 等旧端点。新模型接受文本、图像、音频和视频输入，可生成最长 15 秒、带原生音频的高分辨率视频，并支持多轮对话式自然语言编辑与增强的参考遵循能力。价格较上一代下调 30%，720p 分辨率降至每秒约 0.07 美元。更长的片段加更低的成本，正在把 AI 视频生成推向实际生产可用区间，也给 Sora、Veo 等竞品带来直接价格压力。对做视频评测的团队来说，新一轮基准重测已经在路上。

Google DeepMind released Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash in AI Studio and the Gemini API, replacing older 1.1 endpoints. The model accepts text, image, audio and video inputs, generates high-resolution clips up to 15 seconds with native audio, and supports multi-turn conversational editing through natural-language instructions. Pricing drops 30% versus the previous generation, to about $0.07 per second of 720p video. Longer clips at lower cost push AI video generation closer to practical production use and intensify the video race against Sora and Veo. Teams running multimodal evaluations will want to re-run their benchmarks to see whether cheaper generation holds up in their iteration loops.

METR study: Anthropic models improving 1.9x faster, with 80% of the speedup driven by AI-assisted R&D

独立评估机构 METR 发布了一项为期 11 周（8 月至 10 月）的评估，覆盖 Anthropic 的机密研究与未发布模型。结果显示，Anthropic 模型的能力提升速度约为一年前的 1.9 倍，而主要驱动力不是新增算力，而是 AI 辅助研发本身——内部估计约 80% 的加速来自研发环节使用自家模型。这与此前 Opus 4.7 迭代版本、跨会话记忆与 dreaming 机制研究相互印证。当 AI 开始实质性加速 AI 研发，即使 CEO Dario Amodei 公开呼吁行业减速，进步的节奏也可能比预想更难放慢。递归自改进的临界点正在从假设变成可测量的趋势。

METR ran an 11-week assessment from August through October 2026 covering Anthropic's confidential research and unreleased models. It found capability gains running about 1.9x faster than the prior year, driven mainly by AI-assisted development rather than added compute - internal estimates attribute roughly 80% of the acceleration to AI use in the R&D loop itself. The finding lands alongside Anthropic's cross-session agent memory and dreaming research and its amended S-1A warning that it may soon reach its own definition of recursive self-improvement. If AI meaningfully accelerates AI development, the pace of progress may be harder to slow than industry leaders suggest, even as Dario Amodei publicly calls for restraint.

Anthropic reached 50% compute margins on 7x inference gains - Jamin Ball on why frontier labs need public debt

Latent Space 最新一期邀请云分析机构 Jamin Ball 拆解 Anthropic 的 S-1 文件：尽管账面出现约 807 亿美元 GAAP 亏损（主要由可转债驱动），业务现金流实际为正。核心数据是推理效率——Anthropic 营收同比增长 14 倍，而模型服务算力支出仅增长 2.3 倍，相当于 7 倍的推理效率增益，推理毛利率已达 50%。总算力中只有 27% 在服务客户模型，其余 73% 投向训练、对齐与内部研究。Ball 还论证前沿实验室的数万亿美元物理算力建设，仅靠风投股权无法融资，需要公开债市入场，而债券投资人会要求审计财报与信用评级。算力分配正在成为实验室 CEO 之间的零和博弈。

Jamin Ball joins Latent Space to unpack Anthropic's S-1 filing. Behind the headline $80.7B GAAP loss - driven largely by convertible notes - the business generates positive cash flow. The striking number is inference efficiency: revenue grew 14x year-over-year while model-serving compute spend rose just 2.3x, an effective 7x gain, pushing inference compute margins to 50%. Only 27% of total compute actively serves customer models; 73% goes to training, alignment and internal research. Ball argues that frontier labs face multi-trillion-dollar physical compute buildouts that venture equity alone cannot finance - public debt markets must step in, and credit providers will demand audited filings and ratings. Compute allocation is becoming a zero-sum executive trade-off, with coding offering the highest ROI per token served.
