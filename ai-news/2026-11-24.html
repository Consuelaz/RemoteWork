Z.ai ships GLM-5.4, its first Loop Transformer, matching 2-3T class intelligence at 750B params

11月23日晚，智谱发布 GLM-5.4，首款采用 Loop Transformer 架构的旗舰模型。做法不是从头预训练，而是把已完成预训练的基座改造：对 GLM-5 底座中28层做两轮共享权重的循环，配合三项工程设计——门控渐进更新（第一轮保守建立全局上下文，第二轮放开写回精炼结果）、KV cache 跨轮共享加第二轮滑动窗口、第二轮直接复用首轮的稀疏注意力索引。结果是750B 参数达到2T-3T 级智能水平。30B 同参对照（总训练算力仅增约10%）显示 AIME25 从19.17升到30.42、GPQA-Diamond 从47.58升到56.26、MMLU-Pro 从71.65升到75.05，同时平均输出长度下降10%-14%——部分思维链被内化进了循环深度。泛Coding 能力延伸到用代码生成视频、完整游戏、FreeCAD 六轴机械臂、纯 GLSL 3D 场景。非高峰推理200 token/s，高峰维持70-80。Coding Plan 额度提升至1.5倍，API 待安全评估后开放，权重计划负责任开源。

Z.ai released GLM-5.4 on November 23, its first model built on a Loop Transformer. Rather than pretraining from scratch, the team retrofitted an existing base: 28 layers loop twice with shared weights, supported by gated progressive updates, KV cache sharing across passes plus a sliding window for the second pass, and reuse of the first pass's sparse-attention indices. The result is 750B parameters performing at what Z.ai calls 2T-3T level. On a controlled 30B comparison adding only about 10% training compute, AIME25 rose from 19.17 to 30.42, GPQA-Diamond from 47.58 to 56.26, and MMLU-Pro from 71.65 to 75.05, while average output length fell 10-14%. Generalized coding extends to video, games, FreeCAD robotic arms and GLSL scenes, at 200 tokens/s off-peak and 70-80 at peak.

Bob McGrew, OpenAI's former Chief Research Officer and now CEO of Arda, joins No Priors on November 19 to dismantle the hard-takeoff story. DeepCrossAttention: Supercharging Transformer Residual Connections

Google Research 11月23日的技术博客讲了一件根本性的事：残差连接可能一直在浪费深度。标准残差的固定逐点相加，把所有前序层的信息等权混进同一条残差流，浅层的高分辨率细节在几十层之后被平均掉、被扭曲，后面只能重新算一遍。DeepCrossAttention（DCA）把残差重构成一个动态路由问题：每个 token 先由一个可学习的静态基座加上输入相关投影得到混合权重，再用一套在「层」的维度而非「token」维度上做注意力的 split-QKV 交叉注意力，去查询前序层嵌入的动态加权组合。路由模块只增加不到0.2%的参数，代价转移到显存带宽——前序表示必须原样保留而不能提前混合。在 C4 与 LM1B 上，同等质量最多快3倍、达成同等困惑度最多省5倍训练步数；LM1B 想压到困惑度14以下，标准 transformer 要42层，30层的 DCA 做到了更低，深度与参数双降29%。最实用的性质是可改造：DCA 初始化时能复现普通残差网络，可直接在已有 checkpoint 上续训，困惑度降0.19，而常规续训只降0.02。文中把 Moonshot 的 Attention Residuals、Hyper-Connections 与 Thinking Machines 的 Inkling 2 归为同一簇思路——让「读哪一层、读多少」变成可学的。

A Google Research technical post published November 23 argues that residual connections may have been wasting depth. Standard residuals add every layer's input into one evolving stream with equal implicit weight, so fine-grained early features get averaged away and later blocks must recompute them. DeepCrossAttention reframes residual learning as dynamic routing: each token gets mixing weights from a learned static base plus an input-dependent projection, then split-query/key/value cross-attention operating across depth rather than across sequence positions lets that token query a weighted mixture of its own representations at earlier layers. Routing adds under 0.2% of parameters, with the cost moving to memory bandwidth because earlier representations must stay intact. On C4 and LM1B, DCA matched standard transformers with up to 5x fewer steps, and hit sub-14 perplexity on LM1B with 30 layers versus 42, a 29% cut in depth and parameters. Crucially it can be retrofitted: initialised to reproduce an ordinary residual network, brief continued training cut perplexity by 0.19 versus 0.02 for standard training.
