Anthropic Research @AnthropicAI 前沿 AI 实验室

Claude authors a 70-page VP != VNP proof with a 73,000-line Lean formalization, and no human has checked it yet

继黎曼猜想之后，Anthropic 静默提交了一篇 70 页论文，声称由内部研究版 Claude 独立攻克代数版 P vs NP：在所有域上 VP 严格小于 VNP。这是 1979 年 Valiant 提出猜想以来近半个世纪悬而未决的问题。Claude 把永久式与哈密顿回路多项式的一般算术电路下界从此前的 m^2·log m 量级直接推进到超多项式级别，证明路径极具机器直觉：将张量秩转化为几何距离，再分「平坦」与「收缩」两种情况分别证通，即便它自己也判断不了真实世界属于哪种。配套约 7.3 万行 Lean 4 形式化代码全部通过机器校验，并附独立编写的 nanoda 第二内核交叉验证。但 README 坦言：尚无任何人类数学家检查过该证明，Lean 陈述与论文主张的匹配也仅由 AI 读者判断。

Anthropic quietly uploaded a 70-page paper credited solely to Claude claiming a proof of Valiant's VP != VNP conjecture over every field, the algebraic analogue of P vs NP. The result pushes general arithmetic-circuit lower bounds for the permanent and Hamiltonian cycle polynomial from roughly m^2 log m to quasipolynomial territory, via a striking machine-style proof: tensor rank is converted into geometric distance, then two exhaustive branches (Flat and Contracting) are proven separately. A Lean 4 formalization of about 73,000 lines across 324 files passes machine checks, with an independent second kernel, nanoda, re-validating proofs in CI. The README is candid: no human mathematician has checked the proof, and whether the Lean statements encode the claimed theorem was judged only by AI readers.

Greg Brockman @gdb OpenAI 总裁兼联合创始人

OpenAI and Cisco open-source Shirudo: eBPF shields that block Linux exploits without restarting services

OpenAI 与思科联合发布开源安全运行时 Shirudo（日语「盾」），瞄准漏洞披露到补丁落地之间那段危险窗口。它基于 eBPF 让小型程序运行在内核特定钩子上：Agent 负责调查漏洞、起草针对性防护层（shield）、用真实漏洞利用和负载验证效果，但部署决定权始终留给安全团队。在受控测试中，Codex 生成的 shield 为 Java Base64 解码器加入输入检查并附加到运行中的 Elasticsearch 进程，成功拦截 Java4Shell 攻击且服务全程未重启。信任链设计相当硬核：shield 加载前须经后量子签名 ML-DSA-87 验证，eBPF 验证器强制安全规则，TPM 2.0 记录每次加载/卸载形成可远程核验的审计日志。BSD 2-Clause 开源，这是「AI 写缓解方案、人来批准部署」模式的标志性落地。

OpenAI and Cisco launched Shirudo (Japanese for shield), an open-source runtime that protects running Linux services during the gap between vulnerability disclosure and a permanent patch. Built on eBPF, it lets agents investigate flaws, draft targeted shields, and validate them against real exploits and workloads, while security teams control deployment. In a controlled test, a Codex-generated shield added an input check to Java's Base64 decoder and attached it to a live Elasticsearch process, blocking a Java4Shell exploit without a restart. The trust chain is serious: shields are signed with post-quantum ML-DSA-87, kernel controls restrict unauthorized loads, and TPM 2.0 maintains a verifiable record of protections. BSD 2-Clause licensed.

Vatsal Bhatt @AnthropicAI Anthropic 工程总监

Claude Ship beta lets AI agents review, fix and approve pull requests under team-written natural language policies

当 Claude 写完代码之后，PR 之后的所有环节也交给它？Anthropic 发布 Claude Ship：Code Review 已全面可用，新增两个 Beta Agent——PR Steward 专治 CI 失败、合并冲突和评审反馈，每次 push 后留言说明改了什么、如何回滚，但绝不批准或合并；Claude Approvals 则读取仓库中以自然语言编写的策略文件，像更聪明的 CODEOWNERS 一样自行批准例行变更，敏感场景强制升级人工。官方建议先以 observe-only 模式起步，从单仓库和月度限额开始。数据很有说服力：Claude Code 团队 6 月到 9 月合并 PR 数量翻倍、回滚率下降超 40%。当 AI 生成的代码成为主流，审批自动化就是下一个瓶颈——Enterprise 组织还可申领至少 1 万美元使用额度。

Anthropic launched Claude Ship, a suite of agents covering everything after the pull request. Code Review is generally available; two new beta agents join it: PR Steward fixes failing CI checks, merge conflicts and review feedback (commenting after every push on what changed and how to roll back, but never approving or merging), while Claude Approvals applies an approval policy the team writes as a natural-language file in the repository — like a smarter CODEOWNERS — auto-approving routine changes and escalating sensitive ones to humans. Anthropic recommends starting in observe-only mode with one repo and monthly spend limits. On the Claude Code team, merged PRs more than doubled from June to September while reverted changes fell over 40%. Rolling out to Team/Enterprise on GitHub; enterprises can claim at least $10,000 in credits.

Jakub Pachocki @OpenAI OpenAI 首席科学家

Perplexity and Intelligence: publishing pretraining loss as the industry's shared speedometer and proposing common limits

OpenAI 首席科学家 Jakub Pachocki 发布长文《Perplexity and Intelligence》，首次公开公司内部预训练核心指标，并提议全行业以此为标尺设立「发展上限」——这是「给前沿踩刹车」首次有了可量化的版本。他披露：GPT-3.5 对数困惑度约 0.99、GPT-4 约 0.91，低于 0.8 后模型在网络安全与数学领域涌现变革性能力；约 0.76 时已能接连攻克开放数学难题；他预计进入 0.68-0.7 窗口将出现下一波变革能力，且最早 2027 年 AI 就可能实质性加速预训练研究本身——突破速度可能快于监控与安全技术的跟进。他提议开发者承诺在「理解与监控 AI 行为能力达到具体里程碑（如 CoT 监控的后继技术）」之前不越过共同商定的门槛。Sam Altman 称这是「OpenAI 近期发布的最重要的内容」，Anthropic 的 frontier pacing 主张由此有了统一的度量衡。

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published 'Perplexity and Intelligence', disclosing the company's internal pretraining metrics for the first time and proposing that frontier developers adopt common limits they will not cross until monitoring capability reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors. The disclosed numbers: log-perplexity about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 and 0.91 for GPT-4; below 0.8, transformative capabilities emerged in cybersecurity and mathematics; around 0.76, models began producing notable results on open math problems. He expects another capability jump in the 0.68-0.70 window and warns that AI-driven advances in pretraining research itself could arrive as soon as 2027, potentially outpacing safety monitoring. He is candid about the metric's limits (different datasets, no task mapping, unknown floor). Sam Altman called it the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while; it gives Dario Amodei's 'pace the frontier' idea a measurable yardstick.
