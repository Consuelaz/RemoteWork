Rikiya Takehi @MIT_CSAIL MIT CSAIL researcher

Weight-Space Programming: GPT-6 Astra reads and edits neural network weights by pure reasoning

MIT CSAIL 团队 11 月 2 日发布 Weight-Space Programming 研究：让 GPT-6 Astra 通过纯推理直接读取和编辑其他神经网络的权重，全程不使用梯度下降或训练循环。实验中 Astra 仅凭约 1.7 万个原始权重就以 99% 置信度判断出策略网络训练的游戏是 Breakout；对 8000 万参数的 Qwen3.5-0.8B，它定位并删除了引发推理死循环的方向，只修改约 0.016% 的参数就把 MATH-500 准确率从 7.9% 提升到 48.1%，指令遵循能力同步提升并泛化到其他任务。团队已公开全部日志与实验细节，认为前沿 LLM 已能原生读写神经网络权重，或将重塑优化与可解释性范式。

MIT CSAIL researchers introduced Weight-Space Programming on November 2: GPT-6 Astra reads and edits the internal weights of other neural networks through pure reasoning, with no gradient descent or training loop. In tests, Astra identified the game a small policy network was trained on with 99% confidence from about 17,000 raw weights (Breakout). For the 80M-parameter Qwen3.5-0.8B, it located and removed a specific direction causing reasoning loops, editing only about 0.016% of parameters to raise MATH-500 accuracy from 7.9% to 48.1%, while also improving instruction following and generalizing to other tasks. The team published full logs and experiments, arguing frontier LLMs can now natively read, write and reason about neural network weights - potentially reshaping optimization and interpretability paradigms.
