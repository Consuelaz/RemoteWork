Ena Pragma @enapragma AI engineering field notes

OpenAI and Anthropic agree on what comes after loops — but they agree on less than the video says

这篇 field note 对流传甚广的视频《OpenAI 和 Anthropic 都同意：循环之后是什么》做了逐条事实核查，结论很有价值：两家在 Agent 组织形态的「低层阶梯」上确实一致——单个 Agent 配 skills 的效果持续优于「一个 prompt 包打天下」和子 Agent 方案；任务真正独立、或有严格 checker 时，多 Agent 才有正收益。但「开放 swarm」的高层主张证据薄弱：Anthropic 漏洞狩猎 swarm 在 2700 万 token 的运行中找到 266 个漏洞（对照组 21 个），但约一半发现位于对照组搜索范围之外，并非干净的 12 倍提升。作者的结论值得每个搭多 Agent 系统的人记住：去构建 checker，而不是构建组织架构图。

This field note fact-checks the viral video 'OpenAI and Anthropic Agree on What Comes After Loops' against primary sources, and the result is valuable. Both labs genuinely agree on the lower rungs: a single agent with skills consistently beats both the one-prompt-for-everything design and subagent designs on quality, cost and latency; multi-agent fan-out only pays when work is truly independent or a formal checker exists. But the top rung — open-ended swarms — has thin evidence: Anthropic's vulnerability-hunting swarm found 266 bugs over a 27M-token run versus 21 for plain parallel agents, yet roughly half the finds sat outside the searched scope, so it is not a clean 12x. The takeaway for anyone building multi-agent systems: build the checker, not the org chart.
